## Final steps

<Callout type="warn">
    **Save your key.** Luarmor Ad Reward pages store keys in a browser/IP session. Switching browsers or devices can make the page forget your key. Always copy it somewhere safe after generating or renewing.
</Callout>

<Step number={1} title="Generate or Renew Your Key">
    After being redirected back to **Luarmor**, click **Get a New Key**.

    <Callout type="info">
        If you **already have a key**, click **Renew** instead of creating a new one and skip to **[Get the Script](#get-the-script)** step.
    </Callout>
</Step>

<div className="flex flex-col md:flex-row gap-2 justify-center items-center">
    <StepImage
        src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/1.png"
        alt="Luarmor get new key"
        width={350}
        className="h-[250px] object-contain border-0"
    />

    <StepImage
        src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/renew.png"
        alt="Luarmor renew key"
        width={350}
        className="h-[250px] object-contain border-0"
    />
</div>

<Step number={2} title="Copy the Generated Key">
    Once the key is generated *(or renewed)*, copy it to your clipboard **and keep a backup**.
</Step>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/2.png"
    alt="Copy key"
    width={500}
/>

<Step number={3} title="Link the Key in the Subscription Dashboard">
    Sign in to the <a href="https://www.mspaint.cc/subscription-dashboard">Subscription Dashboard</a> with the same Discord account you want to use for mspaint.

    Click **Link Key System Luarmor key**, paste your key, and submit.

    If your linked free key later **expires** and Renew gives you a different key string, use **Update Key System key** in the Subscription Dashboard (only available while expired).

    <Callout type="info">
        The dashboard links the key to your Discord account automatically. You do **not** need to Redeem the key in the Discord Luarmor panel.
    </Callout>

    <Callout type="warn">
        Ensure there are **no extra spaces** before or after the key.
    </Callout>
</Step>

### Redeem Key in Discord (optional)

Follow this if you want the **Whitelisted** Discord role and script panel features in the server.

Open Discord and go to the [#get-script](https://discord.com/channels/1282361102935658777/1282373591652110417) channel in our [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/mspaint).

<Callout type="warn">
    If you can't find the channel, enable **Show All Channels** option in the server settings.

    <img width="200" height="200" src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/allchannels.png" />
</Callout>

Click the **Redeem Key** button in the script panel, then paste your key and submit.

<Callout type="info">
    **Redeem Key** is not required if you already linked your key in the **Subscription Dashboard**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
    Ensure there are **no extra spaces** before or after the key.
</Callout>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/3.png"
    alt="Redeem key button"
    width={600}
/>

### Get the Whitelisted Role

After redeeming (or if your key is already linked to your Discord account), click **Get Role** in the script panel to receive the **Whitelisted** role.

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/5.png"
    alt="Get Whitelisted Role"
    width={500}
/>

You can get the script from the <a href="https://www.mspaint.cc/subscription-dashboard">Subscription Dashboard</a> after linking, or from the Discord panel: click **Get Script**, scroll down, and paste the script into your executor.
