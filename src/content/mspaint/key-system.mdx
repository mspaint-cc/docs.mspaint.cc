## Final steps

<Callout type="warn">
    **Save your key.** Luarmor Ad Reward pages store keys in a browser/IP session. Switching browsers or devices can make the page forget your key. Always copy it somewhere safe after generating or renewing.
</Callout>

<Step number={1} title="Generate or Renew Your Key">
    After being redirected back to **Luarmor**, click **Get a New Key**.

    <Callout type="info">
        If you **already have a key**, click **Renew** instead of creating a new one. After renewing, go back to the Subscription Dashboard and click **Refresh status** (or **Redeem or link key** if the key changed), then continue to **[Get the Script](#get-the-script)**.
    </Callout>
</Step>

<div className="flex flex-col md:flex-row gap-2 justify-center items-center">
    <StepImage
        src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/1.png"
        alt="Luarmor get new key"
        width={350}
        className="h-[250px] object-contain border-0"
    />

    <StepImage
        src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/renew.png"
        alt="Luarmor renew key"
        width={350}
        className="h-[250px] object-contain border-0"
    />
</div>

<Step number={2} title="Copy the Generated Key">
    Once the key is generated *(or renewed)*, copy it to your clipboard **and keep a backup**.
</Step>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/2.png"
    alt="Copy key"
    width={500}
/>

<Step number={3} title="Open the Subscription Dashboard">
    Sign in to the <a href="https://www.mspaint.cc/subscription-dashboard">Subscription Dashboard</a> with the same Discord account you want to use for mspaint.
</Step>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/dashboard-overview.png"
    alt="Subscription Dashboard overview"
    width={750}
/>

<Step number={4} title="Link Your Key">
    On the Subscription card, click **Redeem or link key**.

    Paste your freemium Key System key into the input, then click **Submit**.

    <Callout type="info">
        If your linked free key later **expires**, prefer **Renew** on the Luarmor Ad page. Then click **Refresh status** in the dashboard. Only use **Redeem or link key** again if Renew gave you a different key.
    </Callout>
</Step>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/dashboard-redeem-or-link.png"
    alt="Redeem or link key button"
    width={750}
/>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/dashboard-paste-key.png"
    alt="Paste key and submit"
    width={600}
/>

### Get the Script

<Step number={5} title="Open Get Script">
    After your key is linked and active, open the **Script** card and click **Get Script**.

    On first use, accept the [Terms of Service](https://www.mspaint.cc/tos) dialog, then the script dialog will open.
</Step>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/dashboard-get-script.png"
    alt="Get Script button"
    width={750}
/>

<Step number={6} title="Copy and Execute the Script">
    Click the copy button in the script dialog, then paste the full script into your executor.
</Step>

<StepImage
    src="/images/mspaint/keytutorial/finish/dashboard-copy-script.png"
    alt="Copy script from dashboard"
    width={750}
/>
